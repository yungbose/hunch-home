# Changes

## 0.2.0

The first release of the Hunch Home app.

- The model, its rules and a page of its own in one app.
- Reads your home by itself: every device exposed to Assist, with its names and room.
- Joins your assistant with one button. A home command is Hunch's: it does it, or says in one short line why
  not. Anything else goes to the agent you had before.
- Teaching: correct a sentence on the page, and Hunch answers it your way from then on.
- Listening with no wake word, experimental and off by default, through a microphone on this machine.
- The model is Hunch-1.1 Home.
