            Hunch listens through a microphone plugged into this Home Assistant machine and acts on the commands it hears, with nothing to say first. Three rules always hold:

            • It never unlocks a lock, and never opens or closes anything but blinds and curtains.
            • It passes nothing on to another agent.
            • It keeps nothing it ignored.
